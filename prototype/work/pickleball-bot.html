The problem and the fix

Problem

The extension needed a browser left open at 7 AM, every day.

What we built

A scheduled job starts a Playwright browser on my own runner, books the best free court, records the attempt and adds it to the calendar.

  1. 1

    cron-jobs.org fires at 7:00

  2. 2

    GitHub Actions starts the job

  3. 3

    Playwright books the best court

  4. 4

    Google Calendar gets the event

My part

I built it alone: the self-hosted runner, the court priority list, a daylight-saving fix and one job per player.
