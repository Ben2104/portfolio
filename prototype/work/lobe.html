The problem and the fix

Problem

Reviewing MRI slices and 3D views with other people usually means one shared screen and a lot of pointing at the wrong spot.

What we built

A MONAI SegResNet model segments the tumour from four MRI sequences. The case then opens as linked 2D slices, a 3D brain and a live review room.

  2. 2

My part

I built the Miro-style 3D whiteboard, fixed dragging, anchoring and undo on it, and polished the motion and the phone layout.
